Questions we hear often

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Stripe emails a receipt with a manage link. Use it to cancel or update billing instantly.

Do I need Facebook?

Community Members gain access to a private Facebook group, but study plans and Faith Formation AI remain on our site.

Is my payment secure?

Stripe handles all payment data. Grounded Through Faith never stores your card information.

Already a Community Member?

Stripe emails your receipt and a unique membership code immediately after checkout. Visit the sign-in page to enter that code, set your password, launch the member portal, and recover links to the private Facebook group and unlimited Faith Formation AI experience.
