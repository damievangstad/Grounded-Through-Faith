Everything fuels the same mission

Grounded Through Faith exists to help men become godly leaders, husbands, fathers, and protectors. Membership dollars cover curriculum development, scholarships, and the pastoral team who moderates the community. Because 100% of proceeds go right back into this mission, every membership literally equips another brother in Christ.

1. Formation resources

Questions we hear often

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Stripe emails a receipt with a manage link. Use it to cancel or update billing instantly.

Do I need Facebook?

Community Members gain access to a private Facebook group, but study plans and Faith Formation AI remain on our site.

Is my payment secure?

Stripe handles all payment data. Grounded Through Faith never stores your card information.

Already a Community Member?

Stripe emails your receipt and login instructions immediately after checkout. Visit the sign-in page to confirm your status, launch the member portal, and recover links to the private Facebook group and unlimited Faith Formation AI experience.
