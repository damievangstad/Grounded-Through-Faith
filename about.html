Our Mission

Helping men center their lives on Christ

Grounded Through Faith exists to raise up godly leaders, husbands, fathers, and protectors who reflect Jesus in every arena of life. Every prayer, resource, and gift fuels discipleship that equips men to walk in holiness for the sake of their families, parishes, and communities.

Why we focus on men

We believe revival begins at home. When men root their identity in Jesus, they become steady husbands, courageous fathers, and servant-hearted leaders who lift entire households toward Christ.

Our formation journeys cultivate integrity, purity, discipline, and sacrificial love. Small groups, mentors, and parish partners walk alongside each brother so he never feels alone in the fight for holiness.

How we serve

Immersive study plans

Multi-week journeys explore identity, leadership, vocation, and perseverance with Scripture, reflection, and prayer that keep Christ at the center.

Personalized devotional tools

Faith Formation AI crafts custom devotionals and study outlines that respond to real-time needs in the lives of men and their families.

Brotherhood & mentorship

We cultivate communities where men pray together, hold one another accountable, and step into mission with courage and compassion.

Stewardship you can trust

Every donation, store purchase, and subscription dollar is directed entirely toward discipling men. 100% of proceeds go straight to mission. We reinvest in resources, retreats, mentorship, and scholarship opportunities that keep Christ at the heart of their lives.

We honor the generosity of our supporters by remaining transparent, prayerful, and accountable. Your partnership fuels transformed families and renewed parishes.
