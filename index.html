New Spiritual Companion

Craft a custom 7-day devotional in seconds

Share a focus, longing, or intention and our on-page guide will prayerfully assemble a week of Scripture, reflection prompts, and spiritual practices to help you stay rooted. Every plan honors Catholic teaching while giving you room to listen to the Holy Spirit.

How the custom agent helps you

  • 1

    Listens to your prompt

    We gather keywords from your focus to highlight a virtue, saintly companion, and encouragement for the week.

  • 2

    Builds a seven-day rhythm

    Each day blends Scripture, a guided reflection, a concrete spiritual practice, and a short prayer to keep you moving.

  • 3

    Offers gentle accountability

    Save or print the plan, revisit it anytime, and refresh the prompt when the Lord invites you into something new.

Tip for group leaders

Ask everyone in your circle to enter the same intention and compare how the prompts inspire different concrete actions. It is a beautiful way to discern together.
