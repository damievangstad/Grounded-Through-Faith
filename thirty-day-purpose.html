30-Day Way of Jesus

Journey through thirty days of Scripture, discernment, and mission to align your whole life with the heart of Christ. Each movement invites surrender, restores identity, and launches you into loving action rooted in the Gospel.

How to travel this path

  • • Set aside 20-30 minutes each day for prayer, reflection, and response.
  • • Gather a companion or small group to share weekly insights.
  • • Close every day by entrusting your purpose to Jesus.

Weekly Movements

The journey is organized into four weekly movements that spiral deeper into Christ's life. Each movement offers seven days of guided rhythm blending Scripture, reflection, and mission.

Week 1 • Surrender & Identity

Return to the Father's love, renounce false identities, and claim who you are in Christ.

Week 2 • Listening & Mission

Hear the call of Jesus, discern your gifts, and take humble steps into Gospel service.

Week 3 • Mercy & Healing

Invite the Lord to heal wounds, reconcile relationships, and expand your capacity for compassion.

Week 4 • Witness & Sending

Stand firm in Christ, testify to His goodness, and craft a sustainable rule of life that keeps Him at the center.

Daily Rhythm

Each day follows a repeatable pattern that keeps your prayer Christ-centered and actionable.

Scripture

Lectio Divina with a passage aligned to the weekly focus.

Reflection

Guided journaling prompts to invite honest conversation with Jesus.

Practice

Concrete acts of surrender, service, or mercy that embody the day’s grace.

Prayer

Short prayers rooted in Scripture to seal your response in Christ.

Weekly Scripture Map

Pray through these anchor passages each week and layer in additional texts the Holy Spirit highlights.

Week 1 Highlights

  • • Luke 5:1-11 – Leaving the nets for Christ.
  • • Romans 12:1-2 – A living sacrifice transformed by renewal.
  • • Psalm 139 – Known and loved by the Father.

Week 2 Highlights

  • • Mark 10:46-52 – Hearing Jesus and springing up.
  • • 1 Corinthians 12:4-11 – Gifts for the mission.
  • • Isaiah 61:1-3 – Anointed to bring good news.

Week 3 Highlights

  • • Luke 7:36-50 – Mercy poured out on the repentant.
  • • Colossians 3:12-15 – Clothe yourselves with compassion.
  • • Psalm 51 – Create in me a clean heart.

Week 4 Highlights

  • • Matthew 28:16-20 – Commissioned to go.
  • • Acts 4:29-33 – Boldness through the Spirit.
  • • Hebrews 12:1-3 – Fixing our eyes on Jesus.

Rule of Life Planner

Use this template during Week 4 to craft a sustainable rhythm that keeps your purpose centered on Christ beyond the 30 days.

Daily Anchors

  • • Morning offering rooted in Scripture.
  • • Midday examen to stay aware of Christ's presence.
  • • Night prayer of gratitude and surrender.

Weekly Practices

  • • Participation in Mass and sacramental life.
  • • Dedicated time for service or evangelization.
  • • Sabbath rest that delights in God and community.
