30-Day Way of Jesus • Aligning Purpose with God's Will

Follow four weekly movements that draw you deeper into surrender, listening, mercy, and witness. Each day offers Scripture, reflection, and practice to center your purpose on Christ.

View the weekly flow

Journey snapshot

Focus
Surrender → Listening → Mercy → Witness
Format
30 days • 4 weekly movements • Scripture + reflection + practice
Great for
Personal retreat, small groups, or parish cohorts

Tip: Pair each week with a simple fast or holy hour to anchor your response.

Daily rhythm

Pray, listen, act, and seal in prayer

Repeat this rhythm each day to stay rooted while you progress through the four movements.

Scripture

Lectio with passages tied to the weekly focus.

Reflection

Journaling prompts that surface desire and resistance.

Practice

Concrete action that embodies the grace of the day.

Prayer

A short surrender prayer to seal the intention in Christ.

Weekly flow

Move through four Christ-centered movements

Stay with each movement for the full week—or longer—before advancing.

How to Use This Journey

  • • Set aside 20–30 minutes daily for prayer, journaling, and action.
  • • Pair each week with fasting or adoration to deepen surrender.
  • • Invite a friend or group to journey together for accountability.
  • • End each week by naming one concrete step you will carry forward.
